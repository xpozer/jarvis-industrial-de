# Compliance Policy

## Norm Text

This repository does not and must not contain protected text from:
- VDE Verlag publications
- DIN standards
- Beuth Verlag publications
- DGUV regulations

Permitted content:
- norm identifiers (e.g. VDE 0100, DIN VDE 0105-100)
- publication dates and version numbers
- user-created summaries and interpretations
- source references (publisher, year, URL to official source)
- example calculations that do not reproduce norm text

## Outputs

All generated outputs (SAP texts, LNW drafts, calculations) are drafts.
Users must review and approve before use.
This tool does not provide legal or compliance advice.

## Disclaimer

Generated outputs do not constitute legal advice, electrical engineering certification
or official norm compliance confirmation. Always verify with qualified personnel and
official norm documents.
